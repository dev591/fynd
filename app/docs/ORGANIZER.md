# Organizer: Fynd (Shopsense Retail Technologies), researched 2026-10-16
Sources: fynd.com homepage; web search (careers.fynd.com/about, jiocommerce.io, YourStory). Self-reported marketing claims; verify before quoting.

**What it is:** "AI native unified commerce" platform (PaaS). Mumbai-based, founded 2012 as Shopsense, rebranded Fynd 2015, acquired by Reliance (2019, majority stake). Jio Commerce is "powered by Fynd".
**Modules:** Online (D2C/B2B/quick-commerce builders, marketplace Konnect, AI PIM, ONDC seller app) · Supply chain (OMS, WMS, TMS, 3PL) · In-store (POS, Kio kiosks, Scan & go, endless aisle, clienteling, Engage loyalty, Onshelf audits) · Manufacturing (Create, Forge, PLM, SourceX, Cortex planning) · AI/dev (Kaily AI agents, Pixelbin, AI Snap, Boltic, GlamAR, Agentic Commerce, Commerce APIs, Fynix, Ratl).
**Key ideas:** one platform for web+POS+marketplace+supply chain; real-time inventory sync; automated order routing; "don't rip and replace" phased migration; integrates with existing ERP/WMS/accounting; Extensions store + Partner program; docs.fynd.com.
**AI:** agents for sales/support, AI product photography, AI catalog enrichment, workflow automation, AR try-on, AI Commerce Co-Pilot.
**Customers:** Reliance Digital/Trends, JioMart, ASOS, Puma, West Elm, Superdry, Being Human, Ultrahuman, Joyalukkas, Kalyan Silks, Nexus Malls, Campus Shoes, etc.
**Proof points to echo:** 300M+ customers, $2.5B GMV, 20K+ stores, 30 countries, 2,300+ brands, 350K+ orders/hour; Campus Shoes 3.6x sales, Pant Project 5x faster checkout, Mi Arcus 35% lower design cost.
**Tone:** confident, benefit-led, "speed, accuracy and scale", care-focused.

## Implications for our entry
- Pick a problem that maps to a Fynd module and use its vocabulary (catalog, OMS, inventory sync, POS, returns, agents). Judges are commerce/retail-tech people.
- Protagonists that fit well: parcels→sorted lanes (OMS/logistics), scattered catalog→grid (AI PIM).
- Show an agentic/AI element honestly (live trace fits "Kaily"/"Agentic Commerce").
- Frame as extension-friendly: works with existing systems via APIs, no rip-and-replace.
- Consider quoting realistic scale (orders/hour) in the Proof chapter, with our own measured numbers.
